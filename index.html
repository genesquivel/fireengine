Welcome to The FIRE Engine

Answer a few quick questions and we'll set up your retirement forecast with sensible defaults. You can fine-tune everything afterward.

Do you know your FIRE number?

If you already know the annual retirement income you want, Quick Setup can use it. If not, Goal Builder will help you shape it first.

Who are we planning for?

This sets up one or two people in your forecast.

When do you want to retire?

The age you'd like to stop working full-time.

What have you saved so far?

Optional — leave blank and we'll start you at $0. You can add detailed accounts later.

How much are you saving?

Optional — total you contribute across all accounts each year. Leave blank for $0.

What's your retirement target?

The annual income you'd like to spend once retired.

Rec: $6k–$8k/yr (Single) or $12k–$15k/yr (Couple).
